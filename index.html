02 — Who we are

More than a
community.

Varanasi Connect brings people together around Varanasi. We create a space where locals, students, creators and curious people can meet, share experiences, discover the city and build things together.

From online conversations to movie nights, photography contests and future offline events, the goal is simple: make it easier to find your people in the city.
