Why Varanasi Connect?

Because a city feels smaller when you know your people.

We started with a simple thought: it should be easier to meet people in your own city.

Whether you have just moved here, are studying in Varanasi, have lived here all your life, or are simply looking for people who share your interests, there should be a place to start a conversation and turn it into something real.

So we are building a community that begins online and keeps finding ways to meet offline — one movie night, creative meetup, game, photo walk and shared idea at a time.

There is always something to join, try, learn or help make happen. Here are a few ways our community comes together.

01 / WATCH

Movie nights

Pick a film, settle in, watch together and talk about the scenes that stay with you. Our community movie nights happen every Saturday.

02 / EXPLORE

Walks & meetups

Get away from the screen, explore the city and meet familiar usernames in real life.

03 / CAPTURE

Photo walks, creative challenges and competitions that celebrate how members see Varanasi.

04 / PLAY

Gaming together

Find teammates, host game sessions and enjoy a little friendly competition.

05 / CREATE

Creative meetups

Make things, share skills, try something new and collaborate with other curious people.

06 / CONNECT

From small experiments to bigger ideas, members can suggest, organise and build things together. The next idea could be yours.
