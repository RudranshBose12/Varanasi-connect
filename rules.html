Community guidelines

A good space
starts with
respect.

These guidelines help keep Varanasi Connect welcoming, safe and enjoyable. By joining the community, you agree to follow them.

12 guidelines

Our server
rules.

Moderators may take action when behaviour harms the community or makes members feel unsafe. If something is wrong, report it through the ticket channel.

01

Be respectful

Treat everyone with dignity. Personal attacks, insults, trolling and harassment of any kind will not be tolerated. Three warnings result in a timeout; five warnings result in a kick. Continued violations after that will result in a permanent ban.

02

No spam or self-promotion

Avoid repeating messages, posting large walls of text or emojis, or mass-pinging members. Keep promotional links, streams and other server invites strictly inside designated channels.

03

Keep it SFW

Do not share adult content, gore or highly explicit material. This applies to text channels, media uploads, direct messages to members, profile pictures and nicknames.

04

Use the right channels

Keep conversations on-topic. Check the channel description if you are unsure where a topic belongs.

05

No malicious content or cheating

Do not share hacks, exploits, cheats, pirated material or harmful links.

06

No hate speech or threats

Discrimination, hate speech, death threats and profanity intended to hurt others are not acceptable. Repeated violations can lead to timeouts, removal or a permanent ban.

07

No promotion of harmful products

Promoting harmful products such as tobacco or restricted medicines is not allowed.

08

Follow Discord's Terms of Service

All members must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

09

Listen to the moderation team

If a moderator asks you to change your behaviour, please cooperate. If you disagree with an action, take it up privately rather than arguing in public channels.

10

Be truthful about yourself

Faking your age or other aspects of yourself in order to cause harm to others, especially minors, will lead to serious consequences.

11

Contact moderators through tickets

Do not ping a moderator for support. Use the Text tickets channel to contact the moderation team.

12

Respect members' DMs and boundaries

Do not mass-DM members without prior interaction. Report inappropriate DMs from members of our server through the tickets channel.

If you see something that breaks these rules, report it to the moderation team through the tickets channel.
