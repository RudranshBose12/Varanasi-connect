Make space

We're here to meet people, do things and create together. A few simple expectations help make Varanasi Connect a place where everyone feels welcome — online and offline.

OnlineAt eventsIn every community space
VCBetter together
01

Respect everyone

Be kind, welcoming and considerate. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate speech and personal attacks have no place here. Respect different people and opinions.

02

Respect privacy

Never share someone's personal details, private messages, photos or contact information without permission. Ask before photographing, recording or posting someone at an event.

03

Respect boundaries

Consent matters. Respect when someone says no, wants space or doesn't want to participate. Never pressure, threaten, stalk or make another member uncomfortable.

04

Keep it appropriate

Keep shared spaces welcoming. No explicit, hateful, threatening or harmful content, spam, repeated promotions, unsolicited advertising or disruptive behaviour.

05

Be responsible

Play fairly, respect others' work and belongings, follow event instructions, respect venues and treat people responsibly in games, collaborations and offline meetups.

06

Help keep us safe

Follow moderator and organiser instructions. Report concerns instead of retaliating. Scams, impersonation, doxxing and serious harmful behaviour can lead to removal.

How these standards are enforced
Moderators and organisers will consider the context, severity and pattern of behaviour. Actions may include a reminder, content removal, restriction from an activity, temporary suspension or removal from the community. Serious safety concerns may result in immediate removal. These standards apply to conduct connected to Varanasi Connect, including interactions that happen off-platform between members.
